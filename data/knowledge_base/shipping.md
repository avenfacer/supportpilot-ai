# Shipping and Delivery

Northstar Shop is a fictional store. This policy is synthetic demo content.

## Processing time

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days after payment is confirmed. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays start processing on the next business day. You will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number once your order leaves our warehouse.

## Shipping options and costs

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and costs $5.99. Standard shipping is free on orders of $50 or more. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99. Express orders must be placed before 12:00 PM Eastern Time to ship the same business day.

## Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses in the United States and Canada. Delivery to Canada takes 5-10 business days and import duties may be charged by the carrier. We do not ship to P.O. boxes for express orders, and we do not currently ship to other countries.

## Tracking your package

Tracking numbers are provided in the shipping confirmation email and in the order status. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to update after the package is handed to the carrier.

## Delayed or lost packages

If your package has not arrived 5 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support so we can open a carrier investigation. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we will send a replacement or issue a refund after review by a support agent.

## Changing the shipping address

The shipping address can only be changed before the order ships. Once a package has shipped, we cannot redirect it.

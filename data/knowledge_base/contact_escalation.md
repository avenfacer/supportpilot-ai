# Contact and Escalation Policy

Northstar Shop is a fictional store. This content is synthetic demo content.

## Support hours

Human support agents are available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time, excluding public holidays. The support assistant is available at any time.

## Contact channels

You can reach the support team through the support chat or by email at support@northstar.example. Email requests receive a first response within 1 business day.

## Talking to a human agent

You can ask to talk to a human agent at any time. The assistant creates a handoff ticket with a short summary so you do not need to repeat yourself. Outside support hours, the ticket is answered on the next business day.

## Escalated issues

Issues involving suspected fraud, unauthorized charges, account security, product safety, or legal matters are always escalated to a human agent. The assistant does not make decisions about these issues.

## Privacy

Support agents will never ask for your full card number or your account password. Only share your order ID and the details needed to resolve your request.

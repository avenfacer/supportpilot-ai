# Warranty and Product FAQ

Northstar Shop is a fictional store. This content is synthetic demo content.

## Warranty coverage

Northstar-brand electronics include a 1-year limited warranty from the date of delivery. Northstar-brand accessories, such as cables, cases, and straps, include a 90-day limited warranty. Products from other brands are covered by their manufacturer's warranty.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, normal wear and tear, or damage caused by unauthorized repairs or modifications.

## How to make a warranty claim

To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order ID, a description of the problem, and photos or a short video showing the issue. A support agent reviews each claim and decides whether to repair or replace the product.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Northstar gift cards. We do not accept cash on delivery or cryptocurrency.

## Gift cards

Northstar gift cards never expire and can be used for any product on the store. Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Price adjustments

We do not offer price matching with other retailers. If a Northstar product drops in price within 14 days of delivery, you can request a one-time price adjustment as store credit.

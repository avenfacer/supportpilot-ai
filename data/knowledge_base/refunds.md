# Refunds

Northstar Shop is a fictional store. This policy is synthetic demo content.

## Refund timeline

After a returned item arrives at our warehouse, it is inspected within 3 business days. Once the return is approved, the refund is issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. Your bank may take additional time to show the refund on your statement.

## Refunds for cancelled orders

If a cancellation is confirmed before the order ships, the full amount, including shipping, is refunded to the original payment method within 3-5 business days.

## What is refunded

Refunds include the price of the returned items. Original shipping costs are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or was the wrong item.

## Store credit

Instead of a refund to the original payment method, you can choose store credit. Store credit is issued within 1 business day after the return is approved and never expires.

## Refund status

Refunds are only issued after a support agent approves the return or cancellation. The support assistant cannot issue refunds directly.
